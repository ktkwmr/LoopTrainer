    Change the key and tempo freely, and loop any section of a song

    Loop Trainer is a free practice player for learning songs by ear. Open an audio file you have, drag over the waveform to choose the part you want to learn, and it repeats that part as many times as you like. The app finds the beats of the song, so the loop points snap right onto the beat. Slow it down to 70% or even 50% and the pitch stays the same, so you can hear every note of a fast guitar solo and play along in the original key.

    Speed up a little on every loop

    Turn on “Speed up on every loop” and the loop starts slow, then gets a little faster each time it comes around: for example 70%, 72%, 74% … up to 100%. You practise the phrase slowly first and reach the real tempo without stopping to change anything. A count-in can click before each loop so you have time to get ready.

    Speed and key for the whole song, saved loops

    You can also simply change the speed or the key of the whole song and listen or play along, without a loop. Shift the key up or down by up to six semitones, together with the speed. Tune to a song recorded a half step down, or move it to fit your voice. The loops, speed, and key are saved for each song in your browser, and you can keep several named loops per song, such as “Intro” and “Solo, first half”. Open the same file next time and you pick up where you left off.

    Files stay on your device

    The audio is decoded and processed only in your browser and is never uploaded. It works with MP3, AAC (m4a), WAV, and other formats your browser can play, on a computer, tablet, or phone. Music from streaming services (Apple Music, Spotify, YouTube) cannot be opened. No sign-up is needed.

    How to use

    1. Open a song – Tap “Open file” or drop an audio file on the waveform. MP3, AAC (m4a), and WAV work in every browser. Music from streaming services cannot be opened.
    2. Choose the loop – Drag across the waveform. To move a flag, tap A or B (it turns blue) and then tap where it should go, or long-press it and drag. While it plays, “A” and “B” set them at the current position. ◀ and ▶ move them to the previous or next beat.
    3. Zoom the waveform – Pinch with two fingers to stretch or shrink the waveform, and move two fingers sideways to scroll. On a computer, use Ctrl + wheel (or pinch on the trackpad) and Shift + wheel. “Zoom to loop” fits the loop to the screen; press it again to see the whole song.
    4. Beats and bars – The app finds the tempo and the beats of the song (♩ ≈ 120 means about 120 BPM), and the loop points snap to the nearest beat. Turn on “Snap to bar starts” to snap to the first beat of each bar instead (◀ ▶ then move by a bar). Bar starts are guessed, so move them with ◀ ▶ under “Bar starts” if they are off, and set the beats per bar for songs in 3/4 or 6/8. If the tempo looks twice too fast or too slow, use ½ or ×2. Turn off “Snap to beats” to place the points freely (◀ ▶ then move by 0.05 seconds).
    5. Slow down – Set the speed between 50% and 150% with the buttons or the slider. The pitch does not change.
    6. Change the key – Move it up or down in semitones (up to ±6). The tempo does not change.
    7. Speed up on every loop – Turn it on and set where to start, how much to add each loop, and where to stop. It goes back to the first speed when you play from the start of the loop.
    8. Count-in – Clicks play before each loop. Enter the song's BPM (at 100%) or tap it in; the clicks follow the current speed.
    9. Saved loops – “+ Save this loop” adds the current loop to the list. Give it a name, and tap its time to go back to it. Everything is saved for each song and comes back when you open the same file.

    Privacy

    • Your audio stays on your device – Audio files are decoded and played only in your browser. They are never uploaded to a server. This app has no accounts, cookies, or ads.
    • Data saved in your browser – For each song you open, the file name and size, the loops, speed, key, and count-in tempo are saved in your browser's storage so they are there next time. The audio itself is not saved. To work offline, the app is saved there too. You can delete them by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.
    • Audio library – When you open the first file, the app loads the SoundTouchJS library from the jsDelivr CDN. Like any web request, this sends your IP address and browser information to jsDelivr. Nothing about your audio is sent.
    • Server logs and page views – This site is hosted on Cloudflare. Standard access logs (such as IP address, time, and the requested page) are kept for operation and security. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views without cookies (the page URL, referrer, browser information, and page load time).
